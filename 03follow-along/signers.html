Who were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include:

Thomas Jefferson
Principal author of the Declaration of Independence. He wrote most of the document and later became the 3rd President of the United States.
John Hancock
President of the Second Continental Congress and the first signer of the Declaration. His large, bold signature became famous.
Benjamin Franklin
A respected statesman, inventor, scientist, and diplomat who helped secure support for American independence.
John Adams
A leading advocate for independence who later became the 2nd President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
A key leader of the American Revolution and organizer of resistance to British rule.
Roger Sherman
The only person to sign all four major founding documents of the United States.
Robert Morris
Known as the "Financier of the Revolution," he helped finance the American Revolutionary War.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
A Maryland statesman and the last surviving signer of the Declaration of Independence.

In total, 56 delegates from the 13 colonies signed the Declaration of Independence in 1776. Among them, Thomas Jefferson, John Hancock, Benjamin Franklin, and John Adams are generally considered the most famous today.
